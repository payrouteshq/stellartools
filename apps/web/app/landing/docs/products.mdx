title: "Products"
description: "The three billing models available on StellarTools and when to use each one"
---

A product is the thing you are selling. It defines the billing model, the price, and for usage-based products, how credits work. You create products once and reuse them across checkouts.

StellarTools has three product types: one-time, subscription, and metered.

---

## One-time

The customer pays once. You get the money. Nothing recurs, nothing needs managing afterward. Good for software licenses, digital downloads, access passes, and any purchase that only needs to happen once.

Create a checkout with a one-time product and the customer pays the fixed price you set.

```typescript
const checkout = await st.checkout.create({
  type: "product",
  product_id: "prod_abc123",
  customer_email: "user@example.com",
});
```

---

## Subscription

The customer is billed on a recurring schedule. You set the price and the billing period: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or a custom interval.

StellarTools renews the subscription automatically at the end of each period. You can cancel, pause, or resume from the API or from the customer portal.

Available periods:

| Period | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| daily | Billed every day |
| weekly | Billed every 7 days |
| monthly | Billed every 30 days |
| yearly | Billed every 365 days |
| custom | Billed every N days, weeks, or months that you define |

When a subscription renews, StellarTools fires a `subscription.renewed` webhook so you know to keep the customer's access active.

```typescript
// Cancel at end of current period
await st.subscriptions.update(subscriptionId, {
  cancel_at_period_end: true,
});
```

---

## Metered (Credits)

The customer buys a credit bundle upfront and spends those credits as they use your product. You define how many credits are in the bundle and what each credit is worth in terms of your measured unit.

When you create a metered product you set:

- Total Credits: how many credits come with the purchase (e.g., 1000)
- Units per Credit: how many of your measured unit equals one credit (e.g., 500 tokens per credit)
- Unit Label: what you are actually measuring (tokens, bytes, API calls, seconds, images, etc.)
- Credits Expiry: how many days before unused credits expire

Your app calls the consumption API whenever the customer does something that uses credits. StellarTools tracks the remaining balance. When a customer is out of credits, you decide whether to block them or let them buy another bundle.

```typescript
// Deduct 5 credits after processing a task
const result = await st.customers.credits.consume({
  customer_id: "cus_xyz",
  product_id: "prod_abc123",
  amount: 5,
});

if (result.balance <= 0) {
  // redirect to purchase more
}
```

Good fits for metered billing: AI products, file processing, API access, anything where what a customer consumes varies per session.

---

## Choosing a type

If you charge once, use one-time. If you charge on a fixed schedule, use subscription. If the amount depends on what the customer actually does, use metered.

You cannot change a product's type after it is created.
